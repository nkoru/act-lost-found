How ACT Lost & Found works

From reporting an item to returning it to the rightful owner, every report follows a clear and secure process.

01

Report

Submit details about the lost or found item, including its description, location, date, and photo when available.

02

Match

The system compares reports using details such as category, color, location, date, and item description.

03

Verify

Ownership details can be checked before a claim is approved, helping protect items from incorrect claims.

✓

Return

Once verified, the item can be returned and the report marked as resolved or successfully returned.

Trust & Safety

Designed for a safer return process.

Reports, possible matches, and ownership claims go through safeguards designed to protect both the item and the ACT community.

Screened

Protection 01

Report screening

Reports can be checked for incomplete information, suspicious content, or details that should not be shown publicly.

Compared

Protection 02

Smart matching

Lost and found reports can be compared using category, color, location, date, and item descriptions.

Verified

Protection 03

Verified claims

Private ownership details can help verify that the person making the claim is the rightful owner.

Privacy protected
